Bella Group

Bella Group: furniture, outdoor living and kitchens on the Costa Blanca

Bella Furniture & Outdoor in Campoamor is part of Bella Group, a Costa Blanca family of companies since 2005 with showrooms in Altea, Rojales and Dehesa de Campoamor.

What is Bella Group?

Bella Group brings together several specialist businesses under one name. Each one has its own showrooms and team, but they share the same approach: good design, personal advice and products made for life on the coast.

Bella Furniture

Indoor furniture and décor for living rooms, dining rooms and bedrooms, in Altea, Rojales and Campoamor.

Bella Outdoor

Garden furniture for terrace, garden and pool, with brands like Life Outdoor Living and SUNS (Suns by Bella in Altea).

Bella Kitchens

Made-to-measure kitchens: personal design with a 3D drawing, built in the workshop and fitted in your home. In Altea and Rojales.

Bella House & construction

Building, property development and an estate agency, with a workshop for built-in furniture.

Also known as

Bella Furniture & Outdoor, Bella Outdoor & Furniture, Bella Outdoor and Furniture, Bella Outdoor, Bella Furniture, Bella Outside or simply Bella. In Campoamor we are Bella Campoamor, also called Haco Bella after the company behind it, Haco Invest SL.

We are part of Bella Group (BellaGroup), together with Bella Altea, Bella Rojales and Bella Kitchens (sometimes written Belle Kitchens). The outdoor brands most often searched with our name are Bella SUNS (Suns by Bella) and Bella Life (Life Outdoor Living): Bella garden furniture, muebles de jardín or, for our Dutch customers, Bella tuinmeubels.

From Dehesa de Campoamor we serve the whole southern Costa Blanca, so many customers look for us as Bella Campoamor, Bella Orihuela Costa, Bella Torrevieja, Bella La Zenia, Bella Cabo Roig, Bella Playa Flamenca, Bella Pilar de la Horadada, Bella Mil Palmeras or Bella Costa Blanca. Further north and in the Vega Baja, Bella Group is also there for you as Bella Altea and Bella Rojales.
