Ask your question
Do you deliver to my home?

Yes. We deliver your furniture to Dehesa de Campoamor, Orihuela Costa, La Zenia, Torrevieja and the surrounding area. Ask us about the conditions for your address.

Can I see and try the furniture before buying?

Yes. In our 2,000 m² store on two floors you can sit down, feel the materials and compare colours in person.

Do I need an appointment to visit the store?

No. You are welcome without an appointment Monday to Friday 10:00–18:00 and Saturday 10:00–14:00. Furnishing a whole house or several rooms? Then we recommend booking a personal appointment: we help only you, calmly and over a coffee.

Which materials withstand the coastal climate?

Sun, salt and wind call for the right materials. In the store we explain which frames, fabrics and finishes hold up best on your terrace, and how to maintain them.

Which languages do you speak?

We help you in Spanish, English, Dutch, Danish and German – in the store, by phone and by e-mail.

Do you also sell online?

No, we don't have an online shop. On the website you can see a selection and ask for information; you buy in the store, where we advise you in person.
